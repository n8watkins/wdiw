# Name ideas — the YouTube usage-analytics extension

> "Watchprint" was a placeholder that leans on *time*. But the product's heart is
> **engagement** — which channels you actually finish vs. abandon, your attention
> on long-form vs. Shorts — computed **live and local**. The name should lead with
> attention / insight, not just a stopwatch. Pick one, riff, or mix.

## Top picks (my lean)
1. **Attune** — "tune in to how you actually watch." Attention-led, brandable, calm.
   `attune.app` / "Attune for YouTube". Pairs nicely opposite TL;DW (decide → reflect).
2. **Watchpulse** — your viewing *pulse* per channel; implies a living, always-on read.
3. **Tally** — it tallies your time + engagement per channel. Short, friendly, concrete.
4. **Replay** — review what you watched; warm and obvious (but common — check trademarks).
5. **Heed** — "pay attention to your attention." Distinctive, short, a little clever.

## The longer list (grouped)

**Attention / engagement-led** (matches the core)
- **Attune** · **Tune** · **Watchpulse** / **Pulse** · **Heed** · **Engaged** ·
  **Attention** / **Attn** · **Tunein**

**Insight / analytics-led**
- **Tally** · **Reckon** · **Watchlytics** · **Tubescope** / **Scope** · **Vista** ·
  **Lens** · **Readout**

**Playful / a counterpart to TL;DW**
- **WDIW** ("What Did I Watch?") · **DIW** ("Did I Watch?") · **Rewatch** ·
  **Hindsight** · **Afterwatch**

**Shorts / digital-wellbeing angle** (since a Shorts quantifier + optional "help me stop" is a differentiator)
- **Scrollback** · **Doomtally** *(too negative for a default, fun as a sub-feature)* ·
  **Surfaced** · **Mindwatch**

## What to check before committing to one
- **Chrome Web Store** name collision/confusion (several "YouTube … tracker/stats" exist).
- **Domain** (`.app`/`.com`) availability.
- **Trademark** — avoid "YouTube"/"Tube" in the *registered* name (Google brand
  guidelines); "for YouTube" as a descriptor in the listing is fine, the brand name
  shouldn't be Tube-based to be safe (so e.g. **Tubescope** is riskier than **Attune**).
- Say it out loud + does it work as a verb/possessive ("my Attune", "check Watchpulse").

## My recommendation
**Attune** if you want a calm, brandable, attention-first identity that reads as the
reflective counterpart to TL;DW. **Tally** if you want something concrete, friendly,
and instantly clear. Both dodge the "Tube" trademark risk.
