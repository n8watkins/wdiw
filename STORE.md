## Detailed description

**WDIW shows you what you've actually been watching on YouTube — privately.**

Everything WDIW measures is computed right in your browser and stored on your device.
There's no account, no servers, and no tracking. Your watch data never leaves your
browser — it's never transmitted, sold, or shared.

WDIW watches what you watch, then turns it into stats you can learn from. See which
**channels** truly own your hours, and how engaged you are with each one: every video is
auto-rated **Engaged**, **Skimmed**, or **Skipped** based on how much of it you actually
watched. Curious about the rabbit holes? WDIW splits **Shorts vs. long-form**, detects
**doom-scroll sessions**, and can nudge you with an optional **daily Shorts limit**
(a toolbar badge plus a popup banner) when you want to cut back.

Two heatmaps reveal your patterns: an **activity heatmap** of the days you watch, and a
**"when you watch" heatmap** showing your time-of-day habits. Browse it all through
per-channel and lifetime dashboards (**Stats**, **Channels**, **History**), or just pop
open the toolbar for a quick **today** card.

Already have history elsewhere? **Import** a TL;DW history export, a WDIW backup, or a
**Google Takeout** `watch-history.json` to bring in years of past viewing. (Takeout
files include titles, channels, and timestamps but no watch durations, so imported
videos count toward your history without an engagement rating.)

**What you get:**

- Per-channel **watch time** — where your hours really go
- **Engagement** auto-rated Engaged / Skimmed / Skipped per video
- **Shorts vs. long-form** split with **doom-scroll session** detection
- Optional **daily Shorts limit** nudge (toolbar badge + popup banner)
- **Activity heatmap** (days watched) and **"when you watch"** time-of-day heatmap
- Per-channel and lifetime dashboards: **Stats**, **Channels**, **History**
- A popup **today** card for an at-a-glance summary
- **Import** from TL;DW, a WDIW backup, or Google Takeout
- **100% local** — no account, no servers, no tracking; nothing leaves your browser

## Single purpose

WDIW analyzes your own YouTube viewing — watch time, engagement, and Shorts habits —
entirely on your device, and presents it as personal dashboards.

## Permission justifications

- **`storage`** — Save your stats and settings locally on your device.
- **Host permission `https://www.youtube.com/*`** — Observe video playback on YouTube
  watch pages to measure your watch time and engagement.

## Data usage / privacy disclosures

For the Chrome Web Store "Data practices" checklist:

- **What is collected:** the videos you watch on YouTube — title, channel, URL,
  timestamps, watch time, and a derived engagement rating. Plus your WDIW settings.
- **Where it lives:** stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`, on your device only.
- **Sold to third parties?** No.
- **Transferred to third parties?** No — except as required to operate or for legal
  reasons, neither of which applies here: WDIW has no servers and transfers nothing.
- **Used for purposes unrelated to the single purpose?** No.
- **Used for creditworthiness / lending?** No.
- **Handling:** processed and handled entirely locally on the user's device; never
  transmitted, sold, or shared. No analytics, telemetry, ads, third parties, or remote
  code.
